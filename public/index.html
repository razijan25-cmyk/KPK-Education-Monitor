Out-of-School Children (OOSC) Crisis Analytics

Recent socio-economic registration surveys indicate approximately 4.7 million children between the ages of 5 to 16 remain out of school within the province. Deep geographical and gender disparities require immediate intervention.

"The Missing Ustaani" & Systemic Imbalances

An analysis of Annual School Census (ASC) data identifies a critical deficit in female instructional staff. Nearly 25% of all public primary schools enrolling girls operate with a single teacher, forcing multigrade teaching methodologies that severely impact instructional quality. The deficit of localized middle schools leads to high drop-out rates post-primary.
